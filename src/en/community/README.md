## Basics

1. When communicating, please follow the relevant laws and regulations. Publishing the following content in any form (including but not limited to direct or indirect mentions and reposts) is prohibited:
    - Illegal content such as pornography, gambling, drugs, terrorism, and violence;
    - Politically or religiously sensitive or illegal statements;
    - Content that violates another person's privacy or impersonates another person;
    - VPNs and other tools, techniques, and methods that can be used to bypass network firewalls;
    - Content that may make people uncomfortable;
    - Promotional content that points to abnormal communities, websites, or other platforms in any form (including but not limited to direct or indirect links, QR codes, and passwords);
    - Other content that violates the relevant laws and regulations.

2. To protect the physical and mental health of minors, you must be at least 13 years old to join this community and take part in the discussion.

3. Publishing files and links that contain malicious content such as viruses and trojans is prohibited.

4. Commercial advertising is prohibited.

5. Posting the same thing along with others ("chanted" replies) is tolerable in moderation, but a single person must not flood the screen or do anything else that may disturb other people's normal conversation. Any one of the following behaviors counts as disturbing other people's normal conversation and will be penalized:
    - Screen flooding, that is, posting more than 3 identical or similar messages within 5 minutes;
    - Calling bot features frequently, that is, actively calling the bot more than 3 times in any form within 10 minutes;
    - Poke flooding, that is, sending more than 3 poke messages to any target within 10 minutes, or sending more than 1 poke message to a single target;
    - Excessive chanting, that is, sending messages that are identical or similar to messages sent by other members within the last 30 minutes, such that the number of identical or similar messages within 30 minutes exceeds 75;
    - Other behavior that may disturb other people's normal conversation.

    Recalled messages also count toward the message numbers above. Timing is based on the message send time, looking back X minutes (including the boundary moment). For example, "within 30 minutes" means the time range counting back 30 minutes from the moment the current message is posted (including the boundary moment).

6. Before inviting a bot into the group, ask a moderator for permission first; otherwise the bot will be removed, and the person who invited it will also be penalized.

    "Bot" means an account that is fully or partly controlled by a program and can respond automatically according to group chat activity. Whether the account is also operated by a real person at the same time does not affect its being considered a bot.

    Applications for new bots are currently suspended.

7. Do not keep pestering for updates.

8. Harassing other community members is prohibited.

9. Discussing LGBTQ-related content is not encouraged.

10. Please communicate in a friendly way; spreading positive energy in the community is encouraged, and spreading negative energy excessively is not appropriate.

11. If you are not sure whether what you want to post violates the rules above, then you had better not post it.

## 1. Purpose of the group
This group is dedicated to building a **friendly, mutual-help, and efficient** communication environment.  
Any topic related to **pornography, gambling, drugs, violence, or political sensitivity** is prohibited.  


## 2. Member management
| **Role** | **Permissions** | **How to apply** |
|----------|----------|--------------|
| Group owner | Full permissions | Fixed |
| Moderator | Kick members, mute members | Appointed by the group owner |
| Regular member | Chat, file sharing | Join directly |


## 3. Prohibited behavior
- **Advertising**: posting advertising in any form (including QR codes and links) without permission.
- **Screen flooding**: sending more than 3 repeated or meaningless messages in a row.
- **Personal attacks**: insults, discrimination, regional slurs, and so on.
- **Political sensitivity**: discussing sensitive topics such as politics and religion.

**Violation handling process:**  
First time → warning  
Second time → muted for 1 hour  
Third time → kicked out and blocked


## 5. File sharing rules
- **Naming format**: `date-topic-uploader` (example: `20250810-walkthrough-Xiaoming.pdf`)
- **Do not upload**: pirated software, cracking tools, virus files.
- **Regular cleanup**: moderators clean up expired files on the 1st of every month.
