# Using the bot

The bot in this group is a **QQ chat bot** built on the [MaiBot](https://docs.mai-mai.org/manual/) ("Maimai") framework: it is driven by a large language model and joins the conversation much like a person would — it keeps track of the context, and its answers are **generated on the spot**, not looked up in some prepared document.

> [!important] The short version
> **No more than 3 manual calls within one minute**; keep personal information away from it; what it says is not the group owner's or a moderator's position.

## How to call it

- **@ it** in the group, or call it by name — the exact trigger is whatever the group announcement says.
- After being triggered it decides for itself whether and what to answer, so **it will not reply to every message**.
- It only lives in this group and **will never private-message you**: any account that does so in its name is not it — tell a moderator.

## Frequency: no more than 3 calls in one minute

- "One call" means **a person actively calling it**: @-mentioning it, saying its name, sending it a command. It speaking up on its own is not your call.
- Actively calling it **more than 3 times within one minute** counts as disturbing other people's normal conversation and is handled per the [community rules](./README): first time a warning, second time a 1-hour mute, third time kicked out and blocked.
- Typical ways people trip over this: firing off a dozen follow-up questions, using it as a translator / search engine / calculator, or two people chain-calling it at each other.
- If you have several things to ask, **put them in one message** — that is far faster than sending them one by one.

## What not to let it touch

- **Personal information**: real name plus student ID, passwords and keys, ID card numbers, home addresses, screenshots of other people's chats.
  Behind the bot is a third-party LLM service, so whatever you send leaves this machine.
- **Automation**: do not script it to post on a schedule, to advertise, or to pull people in, and do not use it to flood or chant.
- **Treat it as authoritative**: it getting things wrong or outdated is normal. For installing, deploying and configuring ClassShout, the [documentation](/en/guide/) and what the program actually does are what count — do not argue with people using the bot's answer.

## Inviting and hosting bots

- Before bringing a bot into the group you must get the group owner's or a moderator's permission, or the bot will be removed and the person who invited it penalized as well.
- **Applications for new bots are currently suspended** (see basic rule 2 in the [community rules](./README)).
- Want to run one of your own? See the [MaiBot documentation](https://docs.mai-mai.org/manual/). It has no affiliation with this project — this group simply uses it as its group bot.

## When it misbehaves

- **No reply**: model responses take a moment, so wait a few seconds; if nothing happens, say it again (mind the 3-calls-per-minute limit).
- **Answer misses the point**: rephrase the question; it is a chat model, so a vague question only makes it guess.
- **Clearly wrong or out of line**: send a screenshot to a moderator, **do not pile on**, and do not go probing its boundaries.
